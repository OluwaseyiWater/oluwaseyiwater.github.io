I am a first-year PhD student at the <a href="https://www.ece.utoronto.ca/">Edward S. Rogers Sr. Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering, University of Toronto</a> advised by <a href="https://www.ece.utoronto.ca/people/adve-r/">Raviraj Adve</a> and <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/ammarhus">Hussein A. Ammar</a>.

My research is currently on non-terrestrial networks (NTNs) and cell-free networks, funded by <a href="https://www.ericsson.ca/fr">Ericsson Canada</a>. We are currently investigating how satellite and terrestrial access points can transmit phase-coherently with one another.

Prior to this, I obtained my MSc at the <a href="https://www.uct.ac.za/">University of Cape Town</a>, where I was blessed with amazing mentors: Professor <a href="https://shocklab.net/">Jonathan Shock</a> and Dr. <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/jacodutoit/">Jaco Du Toit</a>. In addition to this, I was also fortunate to have worked with Dr. <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=at4MvYMAAAAJ&hl=en">Muhammad Ali Jamshed</a> from the <a href="https://www.gla.ac.uk/">University of Glasgow</a>. Before that, I graduated as the best student from the Department of Electrical Engineering at <a href="https://portal.oouagoiwoye.edu.ng/">Olabisi Onabanjo University</a> with a departmental CGPA record for my undergraduate degree.
